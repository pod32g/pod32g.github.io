## The app

Omni Auth generates time-based (TOTP) and counter-based (HOTP) one-time passwords on your device.
It is a tool for storing and using two-factor authentication secrets that you obtain from other
services.

## Your responsibility for your data

This is the most important thing to understand:

- Your two-factor secrets are stored **only on your device**, encrypted. We have no server and no
  copy of them.
- **If you lose your device and have no backup, your codes cannot be recovered — by you or by us.**
  There is no password reset and no support channel that can restore them.
- You are responsible for keeping a Recovery Kit, an encrypted backup, or your original recovery
  codes from each service.
- If you set a backup password or generate a Recovery Key, you are responsible for storing it
  safely. We cannot recover it, and without it the corresponding backup is permanently unreadable.
