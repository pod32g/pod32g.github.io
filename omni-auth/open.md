# Open Omni Auth

If Omni Auth is installed on this device, this link opens it.

If you are reading this page, it isn't installed yet — or your browser opened the page instead of
the app.

- [Omni Auth on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/omni-auth/id6797746360)
- [About Omni Auth](/omni-auth/)

Nothing about your accounts is in this link. Omni Auth keeps every secret encrypted on your own
device, and a link like this one only opens the app — it can't add, read or change an account.
